This corpus is the data for the paper [A psycholinguistic database for traditional Chinese character naming](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-014-0559-7).  We convert the traditional characters (3314) to simplified using Open-CC.  The characters `蹻 紬` do not simplify easily and were omitted.  The characters were sorted in Unicode order and have duplicates removed, leaving 3253 characters.
