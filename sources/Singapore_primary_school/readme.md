We download and extract the Chinese characters listed in Singapore's Ministry of Education's 《欢乐伙伴》小学华文生字表 (2015; [pdf](https://www.moe.gov.sg/-/media/files/primary/2015characterlistprimarychinese.pdf)) which lists Chinese characters at different levels of primary school.  This gives a list of 1655 characters, all of which were included in MteH v0.1.1.
