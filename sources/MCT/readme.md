The [Medical Chinese Test](https://www.chinesetest.cn/MCT) (MCT) is described as:

>  The Chinese Proficiency Test（Medical）/MCT is aimed at international students going to study medicine in China, as well as healthcare workers who use Chinese as a second language for clinical diagnosis and treatment within and outside China. The test is used to evaluate the test takers'ability to communicate with patients, medical workers, and relevant people in the medical field in Chinese. 

The vocabulary list (1500 words) was downloaded from chinesetest.cn and the characters therein were extracted using `grep -oP '[\p{Han}]' MCT_vocab_original.txt` giving `MCT_chars_unicode_order.txt` (1180 chars).
