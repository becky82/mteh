The 现代汉语通用字表 (2013) character corpus contains 8105 chracters (split into three levels, respectively containing 3500, 3000, and 1605 characters), originally published by the [Ministry of Education](http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/ziliao/A19/201306/t20130601_186002.html) in 2013; it was sourced from [WikiSource](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E9%80%9A%E7%94%A8%E8%A7%84%E8%8C%83%E6%B1%89%E5%AD%97%E8%A1%A8).
