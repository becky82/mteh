Chih-Hao Tsai has a [Chinese character corpus](https://technology.chtsai.org/charfreq/) sourced from Usenet newsgroups during 1993-1994.  It includes both simplified and traditional characters.

From the top-4500 characters therein, [OpenCC](https://github.com/BYVoid/OpenCC) was used to convert the traditional characters to simplified characters, which left 4329 unique characters, namely `Tsai_chars_top4500_simplified_unicode_order.txt`.
