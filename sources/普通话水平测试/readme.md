The 普通话水平测试, or [Putonghua Proficiency Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putonghua_Proficiency_Test), tests spoken Mandarin fluency, designed for native speakers.

The word list was sourced from pthxx.com: [level 1](http://www.pthxx.com/b_audio/03_zici_1/index.html) (6595 words) and [level 2](http://www.pthxx.com/b_audio/04_zici_2/index.html) (10460 words).  The characters therein were extracted.  Level 1 has 2374 distinct characters and level 2 has 1414 distinct characters that did not appear in level 1, giving a total of 3788 characters.
