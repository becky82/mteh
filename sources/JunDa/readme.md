The 4500 most frequent characters were sourced from [Jun Da's modern corpus](https://lingua.mtsu.edu/chinese-computing/statistics/char/list.php?Which=MO).  [OpenCC](https://github.com/BYVoid/OpenCC) identified these traditional characters (15) and converted them to simplified:

> 乾 後 徵 捱 摺 於 榘 經 與 藥 這 過 醫 體 麽

This resulted in a list of 4485 unique simplified characters `JunDa_modern_top4500_simplified_unicode_order.txt`.
