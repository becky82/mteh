You'll encounter province abbreviations in the names of types of cuisine, e.g.:

> ![餐饮 烤鱼川湘家常菜](./misc/烤鱼川湘家常菜.jpg)
>
> <sub>餐饮 烤鱼川湘家常菜</sub>

Here 川 is the abbreviation for 四川 (Sichuan) and 湘 is the abbreviation for 湖南 (Hunan).
